Privacy Policy for Browser Lock

Browser Lock asks you to sign in with a one-time code sent to your email address before the browser can be used.

Data we collect

Your email address, which we use to send the sign-in code.
Authentication data, including the one-time code you enter and a session token that keeps you signed in on your device.

How the data is used

Your email address is used only to send you a sign-in code.
The session token is used only to check whether your sign-in is still valid.

Where the data goes

Requests are sent to our backend, which runs on Cloudflare Workers.
Sign-in emails are sent through Resend.
The extension does not send browsing history, page content, or other personal information anywhere.

Storage
